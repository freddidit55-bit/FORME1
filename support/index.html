Help and support

Contact us

Email the address at the bottom of this page. We usually reply within a few days. For a problem in the app, say which screen you were on and what you tapped. In the app, Settings, Help and legal, Copy diagnostics for support copies details that help us find the cause (no workout data or passwords).

Your membership

FORME is free for 2 weeks, then £5.99 a month or £49.99 a year. It renews automatically until you cancel. Everything in the app is included.

Cancel on iPhone

If you subscribed in the iPhone app, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, FORME, Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Cancel on the website

If you subscribed on the website, open FORME, then Settings, Account and plan, Manage or cancel membership.

Refunds

For iPhone purchases, refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the FORME purchase. For website purchases, email us.

Restore a purchase

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open the membership screen and tap Restore purchases. On the website, log in with the same email you subscribed with.

Moving to a new phone

Log in with the same email on the new phone and your training comes back. Without an account, use Profile, Your data, Download a backup, then Restore backup on the new phone.

Delete your account

In the app, Settings, Account and plan, Delete account and cloud data. This removes your account and the copy of your data on our server, and cancels a website membership. An iPhone membership has to be cancelled separately in your iPhone's Settings.

Pain or injury

FORME gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If an exercise hurts, stop. Tap Can't do this?, then It hurts, choose where, and FORME swaps in moves that leave that area alone. For sharp pain, swelling, or pain that lasts, see a doctor or physio.

Reminders

Settings, Reminders and widgets. On iPhone, reminders also need notifications allowed for FORME in your iPhone's Settings.

Cookies and device storage

Last updated November 2026

No tracking cookies

FORME doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and it doesn't follow you around other websites or apps. That's why there's no cookie banner: the law only asks for your consent to storage that isn't needed for the service you're using.

What FORME keeps on your device

Your training, food and settings (so the app works without an internet connection); your sign-in, so you stay logged in; a short-lived copy of whether your membership is active; small memories like which tab you last had open, which notices you've closed and which workouts were already shared; and offline copies of the app itself, so it opens quickly. These are all needed for things you asked FORME to do. They stay on your device and you can remove them by erasing your data in Profile, signing out, or clearing the site's data in your browser.

Crash reports

If crash reports are on, FORME reads basic technical details (the app version and the browser or phone type) when something breaks, so we can fix it. You can turn this off in Settings, Support and legal.

Other companies' pages

When you go to Stripe to pay, to Google to sign in, or to Strava to connect it, you're on their website and their cookie policies apply there.

Refunds and cancelling

Last updated November 2026

Free trial

New members get 14 days free. Cancel before the trial ends and you are never charged.

iPhone app

Apple takes payments made in the iPhone app and handles their refunds; we can't refund them ourselves. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Thenly, at least 24 hours before it renews.

Website: your 14-day right to cancel

If you change your mind, email us within 14 days of your first payment (the first charge after your free trial) and we'll refund that payment in full, without taking anything off for the days you used Thenly. We'll refund it to the card you paid with within 14 days of hearing from you.

Renewals

Membership renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in Settings, Account and plan, Manage or cancel membership, and you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for. Unused time on a renewal isn't refunded, except where the law gives you a right to cancel after a renewal, or where Thenly didn't work as it should.

Charged by mistake

If you were charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or Thenly didn't work as it should and we couldn't fix it, email us and we'll put it right, including a refund where that's fair.

How to ask

Email the address below from the email address on your account, and say which payment it's about.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

FORME is run by its developer. You can contact us at freddidit55@gmail.com. We are the "controller" of the personal data described here, which means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for it.

What FORME stores on your device

Your name, age (if you add it), goals, equipment, workouts, personal records, notes, food entries, any injuries or illness you tell FORME about, and your settings. By default all of it stays on your device, in this app's own storage. Nothing leaves your device unless you create an account or turn on one of the features below.

Health information

Workouts, injuries, illness, food and an optional body weight are health information, which the law protects more carefully. On your device it is yours. If you create an account, we only store it with your account because you explicitly agree to that when you create the account (you tick a box saying so). You can withdraw that agreement at any time by deleting your account, which deletes the copy on our server.

Accounts and cloud sync

If you create an account, your email address and a copy of your FORME data are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in the European Union (Paris), so you can restore it on another device. Passwords are handled by Supabase and are never visible to us in plain text. If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your email address and confirms it's yours.

Reminders when the app is closed

If you turn this on, your reminder time, training days, first name, main goal name and streak are stored with your account so our server can send the reminder, and your phone's or browser's push address is stored so it knows where to send it. Reminders read like "Time to train, Sam" and can mention your streak or goal, and they can show on your lock screen. Turning reminders off, signing out or deleting your account removes this.

Photos, videos and friend challenges

Skill check-in photos and videos stay on the device you took them on. They are not included in backups or cloud sync, and erasing your data removes them. A friend challenge is a link containing an exercise, a number and your first name; FORME doesn't keep a copy of it anywhere.

Food barcodes

When you scan or type a food barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product's label. Nothing about you is sent with it. The camera picture never leaves your phone. Foods you add yourself, and everything you log, stay with the rest of your FORME data.

Leagues and duels

If you join the league or duel someone, the nickname you choose, your training level, your weekly league XP and your duel results are stored with your account and shown to other players. Nobody sees your email, name or anything else. Under-18s can only duel friends, not join leagues. Deleting your account removes all of it.

Friends

If you add friends (with a code you swap with each other), you and they see each other's nickname and, only if you turn on "Share my finished workouts with friends", each shared workout's name, how many minutes it took and any new personal bests, plus kudos. Shared posts are shown for 14 days and deleted after 60. Nobody else sees them. You can remove a friend or stop sharing at any time, and deleting your account removes all of it.

Strava

If you connect Strava, FORME stores the access keys Strava gives us (never your Strava password) so it can add your finished workouts to your Strava account: the workout name, start time, duration, and the exercises and number of sets. Strava's own privacy policy then applies to that activity. You can turn automatic posting off or disconnect in Settings, which deletes the keys and asks Strava to remove FORME's access; deleting your FORME account deletes them too.

Payments

On the website, payments are processed by Stripe. In the iPhone app, Apple takes the payment through your Apple ID, and RevenueCat (a subscription service) tells us whether your membership is active, using your account's ID. We never see or store your card number. We keep your subscription status, plan and renewal date, and Stripe keeps payment records for as long as tax law requires (6 years in the UK).

Crash reports

If crash reports are switched on and something goes wrong in the app, FORME sends a short technical report to Sentry, our crash-reporting service, so we can fix it: the error message, the app version, whether you're on the website or the app, and the time. It never includes your name, email address, training data, food or photos, and it isn't linked to your account. You can turn crash reports off in Settings, Support and legal.

Why we're allowed to use your data

To give you the service you asked for (your account, sync, your membership, reminders, leagues, friends and Strava when you turn them on): this is needed for our contract with you. Health information in your account: your explicit consent. Crash reports and keeping FORME secure: our legitimate interest in a working, safe app, which you can object to by turning crash reports off. Payment records: a legal obligation.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run FORME for us, each under a contract that limits what they can do with your data: Supabase (accounts, cloud copy, leagues, friends, reminders), Stripe (website payments), Apple and RevenueCat (iPhone payments), Sentry (crash reports, if on), Google (only if you sign in with Google), Strava (only if you connect it), Open Food Facts (a barcode number only), push services run by Apple, Google or your browser maker (to deliver reminders), and GitHub or Netlify, which host the website and keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for a short time. We never sell your data, show ads or share it with advertisers.

Outside the UK

Your cloud copy is stored in the European Union, which the UK recognises as protecting data to the same standard. Some services (Stripe, Sentry, Apple, Google, Strava) may process data in the United States; they do so under the UK's approved safeguards, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contract clauses.

How long we keep it

On your device: until you erase it or delete the app. Your account and cloud copy: until you delete your account, when they are deleted straight away (any copy in our provider's routine database backups disappears when those backups expire). Friends' posts: 60 days. Crash reports: up to 90 days. Payment records: as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit or stop using it, object to how we use it, and take it to another service (Profile, Your data, Download a backup gives you all of it as a file). Where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. Email us and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

Decisions made by the app

FORME adjusts your workouts automatically from what you log (for example, moving you up a stage). These adjustments only change your training plan; nothing about you is decided in a way that has legal or similarly serious effects.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers. FORME doesn't track you across other apps or websites and uses no analytics or advertising cookies. See Cookies and device storage for exactly what is kept on your device.

Your choices

You can export a backup of your data, import it again, or erase everything from Profile at any time. To delete your account and its cloud copy, use Delete account under Settings, Account and plan. That also cancels a subscription bought on the website and disconnects Strava. A subscription bought in the iPhone app is managed by Apple, so cancel it in your iPhone's Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Children

FORME is for people aged 13 and over, and you must be 13 or over to create an account. If you are under 18, please use FORME with a parent or guardian's agreement. For under-18s, FORME keeps the most private settings by default: no calorie goals, no public leagues (duels with friends only), and nothing shared with friends unless they turn it on. If you think a child under 13 has given us their data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Your cloud copy is protected so only your account can read it, and other people only ever see what's described above.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you and, where needed, ask you to agree again.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

What FORME stores

Your name, goals, equipment, workouts, personal records, food entries and settings. By default all of it stays on your device, in this app's own storage. Nothing leaves your device unless you create an account.

Accounts and cloud sync

If you create an account, your email address and a copy of your FORME data are stored with our database provider so you can restore it on another device. Passwords are handled by the provider and are never visible to us in plain text.

Reminders when the app is closed

If you turn this on, your reminder time, training days, first name, main goal name and streak are stored with your account so a server can send the reminder, and your phone's push address is stored so it knows where to send it. Reminders contain only a short generic message. Turning it off, signing out or deleting your account removes them.

Photos, videos and friend challenges

Skill check-in photos and videos stay on the device you took them on. They are not included in backups or cloud sync, and erasing your data removes them. A friend challenge is a link containing an exercise, a number and your first name; FORME doesn't keep a copy of it anywhere.

Food barcodes

When you scan or type a food barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product's label. Nothing about you is sent with it. Foods you add yourself, and everything you log, stay with the rest of your FORME data.

Leagues and duels

If you join the league or duel someone, the nickname you choose, your weekly league XP and your duel results are stored with your account and shown to other players. Nobody sees your email, name or anything else. Deleting your account removes them.

Friends

If you add friends (with a code you swap with each other), you and they see each other's nickname and, only if you turn on "Share my finished workouts with friends", each shared workout's name, how many minutes it took and any new personal bests, plus kudos. Shared posts are shown for 14 days and deleted after 60. Nobody else sees them. You can remove a friend or stop sharing at any time, and deleting your account removes all of it.

Strava

If you connect Strava, FORME stores the access keys Strava gives us (never your Strava password) so it can add your finished workouts to your Strava account: the workout name, start time, duration, and the exercises and number of sets. Strava's own privacy policy then applies to that activity. You can turn automatic posting off or disconnect in Settings, which deletes the keys and asks Strava to remove FORME's access; deleting your FORME account deletes them too.

Payments

On the website, payments are processed by Stripe. In the iPhone app, Apple takes the payment through your Apple ID, and RevenueCat (a subscription service) tells us whether your membership is active, using your account's ID. We never see or store your card number. We keep only your subscription status, plan and renewal date.

Crash reports

If something goes wrong in the app, FORME may send a short technical report to Sentry, our crash-reporting service, so we can fix it: the error message, the app version, whether you're on the website or the app, and the time. It never includes your name, email address, training data, food or photos, and it isn't linked to your account.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers. FORME doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and it uses no analytics or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Staying signed in uses your browser's own storage on your device.

Your choices

You can export a backup of your data, import it again, or erase everything from Profile at any time. To delete your account and its cloud copy, use Delete account under Settings, Account. That also cancels a subscription bought on the website. A subscription bought in the iPhone app is managed by Apple, so cancel it in your iPhone's Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Children

FORME is not designed for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's agreement.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you.

Pictures, demos and sounds

FORME uses no stock photos. The exercise demos, hand diagrams, muscle maps, boss pixel art, logo and icons for the app are drawn by FORME's own code. Screenshots and the promo video are of the app itself.

Coach voice

The coach voice is generated with Kokoro, an open text-to-speech model released under the Apache License 2.0.

Fonts

Barlow and Barlow Condensed by Jeremy Tribby, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Icons

Lucide icons, used under the ISC License.

Food data

Ingredient and basic food numbers come from the USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Release 28 (public domain). Packaged food labels come from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0); product images are not used.

Barcode scanning

ZXing ("Zebra Crossing"), used under the Apache License 2.0.

Open-source software

FORME is built with open-source software including React, TanStack, Radix UI, Tailwind CSS, Recharts and Capacitor, used under the MIT, Apache 2.0 and ISC licences. The full list, with every licence's text, is in third-party-licences.txt on the FORME website.
